# Cashora AI

**Tagline:** Predict Cash. Prevent Shortfalls. Grow Smarter.

## 1. Problem Statement

Small businesses often struggle to manage their cash flow effectively. Even when a business generates sales, delayed customer payments, recurring expenses, and unexpected costs can lead to cash shortages.

Many small businesses rely on manual records or basic spreadsheets to track income and expenses. These methods may not provide timely warnings about future cash shortages or help business owners evaluate financial decisions before taking action.

Therefore, there is a need for an affordable and easy-to-use platform that helps small businesses monitor their cash flow, anticipate potential shortfalls, and make informed financial decisions.

## 2. Proposed Solution

Cashora AI is an AI-powered cash flow prediction and decision support platform designed for small businesses.

The platform allows business owners to record income and expenses, monitor upcoming payments, and analyze their financial position through an interactive dashboard.

It aims to use historical financial records and upcoming payment information to estimate future cash flow, identify potential cash shortages, and provide actionable insights.

Users can also explore hypothetical financial scenarios to understand how changes in expenses, income, or payment timing may affect their available cash.

## 3. Key Objectives

- Track business income, expenses, and cash balances.
- Identify overdue invoices and upcoming payments.
- Visualize cash flow using charts and summaries.
- Estimate future cash balances and flag potential shortfalls.
- Generate AI-assisted financial insights and suggestions.
- Compare hypothetical financial scenarios before making decisions.
- Provide a simple, accessible interface for small business owners.

## 4. Proposed Features

1. **Dashboard:** Overview of available cash, income, expenses, and financial trends.
2. **Transaction Management:** Add, view, edit, and delete income and expense records.
3. **Payment Tracking:** Monitor customer payments, overdue invoices, and upcoming bills.
4. **Cash Flow Forecasting:** Estimate future cash balances using historical data and known upcoming payments.
5. **AI Insights:** Explain spending patterns and highlight potential financial risks.
6. **What-If Analysis:** Compare possible financial scenarios.
7. **Alerts:** Highlight projected cash shortages and important upcoming payments.
8. **Data Management:** Support manual entry and CSV import/export.

## 5. Target Users

- Small business owners
- Retail shops
- Freelancers and independent service providers
- Small agencies and service businesses

## 6. Expected Outcome

Cashora AI aims to help small business owners understand their financial position, anticipate potential cash shortages, and make better-informed cash management decisions.

The platform will provide estimates and decision-support insights, not guaranteed financial predictions or professional financial advice.

## 7. Proposed Technology Stack

- Frontend: React
- UI and development assistance: Lovable
- Database and authentication: Supabase
- AI integration: Secure server-side AI API integration
- Version control: Git and GitHub
- Deployment: Vercel

*The technology stack may be adjusted during development.*

## 8. Data Privacy and Security

The initial version will use manually entered or sample financial data. Users will not be asked to provide bank passwords, card PINs, or banking login credentials. Authentication, access controls, and secure handling of financial records will be implemented as part of development.
